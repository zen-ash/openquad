Pryor St, Decatur St and Kimball Way) from. Everything here is reference only: no photos are
in the repo, the measurements are my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Plan**: osm's box is right (the roof on the satellite is within a meter), and it's the
  face of the marble. Seven bays on Pryor St and Kimball Way, five on Decatur St and the south
  side, 6.1-6.2m each.
- **Height**: 36.05m to the top. The soffit over the ground floor is level, but the ground
  drops 4.3m round the block (usgs): the game's flat ground is the average, Decatur St at
  the middle of the block. Gsu's 2021 photo has 31.55m from the soffit to the top (the fins
  give its scale). Overture's 26m is an estimate and wrong.
- **Up the building**: the ground floor to the soffit at 4.5m, then nine rows of windows 3.3m
  apart (1.45m of glass, 1.85m of marble), the first sill 1.75m over the soffit, and a 1.95m
  band of marble at the top with a thin coping. Ten floors, like gsu says.
- **Bays**: four panes to a bay with thin aluminium mullions, the glass a little behind the
  marble. Each spandrel is four slabs of white marble, the joints on the mullion lines. A
  marble fin about 0.3m wide stands out on every bay line, and a square post on every corner
  goes from the sidewalk to the top.
- **Ground floor**: set back about a meter under the overhang on the three streets (measured
  on the 2019 photo from the Decatur/Kimball corner: 0.8-1.0m), with marble piers under the
  fins out at the face. Kimball Way and Decatur St: navy glazed brick with low black vents and
  a strip of dark windows under the soffit, black louvres in the bay at the Pryor St corner,
  the loading dock's roll up door, white marble panels and the back door (with a small blue
  canopy) at the south end of Kimball Way. Pryor St: grey slatted screens out between the
  piers (staggered slats), louvres behind the first one, the lobby's glass under the canopy.
- **Entrance**: a gsu blue canopy from 12.5 to 23.3m along Pryor St from Decatur St, 2m out
  past the marble with a 0.6m fascia (fitted on the photo from Park Place, the far end from
  the one on Pryor St). Four sliding doors and a sidelight between thin white piers under the
  middle of it, no marble pier in front of the doors.
- **Signs**: "COLLEGE OF EDUCATION & HUMAN DEVELOPMENT" on a white plate over the doors, on
  the marble just over the soffit, and a smaller one on the brick near the Kimball Way corner.
  Blue banners on the Pryor St corner and three fins, and the round blue sign on its bracket.
  All of them have gsu's logo, which is left out (a plain blue square, plain blue).
- **Roof**: a box of dark glass in a white frame, 6.3m over the parapet, near Pryor St (gsu's
  2021 photo has its side toward Kimball Way over the parapet). The rest of the roof is grey.
- **Colors** (ratios inside each photo, the shade ones): the marble a bit warm white, the navy
  brick about 0.1 of it (linear), the soffit nearly black from the street. Windows reflect the
  sky, light grey in the overcast 2019 photos, dark in the sunny 2016 one.

## Not verified

- The ground floor's depth (0.8-1.0m, from the soffit in one photo) and the fins' depth (0.15m,
  by eye).
- The canopy's depth: the photo from Park Place fits a 2m deep canopy from 12.5 to 23.3m as
  well as a 1.5m one from 14 to 26m, and only the far end is in a second photo. Where the doors
  are under it (gsu's 2026 photo is too close to tell) and the piers next to them.
- What's behind the screens on Pryor St (dark glass here), and the bay at the Pryor/Decatur
  corner, which looks like louvres set back with a screen over the end of it.
- The dock (the marker on gsu's map and one close photo: here it's a roll up door in the wall,
  the real one is a bay set into the building) and where the back door is (±2m).
- The box on the roof: the satellite's dark patch and the 2021 photo don't quite agree, it's
  1-2m off one way or the other. Its top was fitted on the one photo.
- The south side above 40-42 Pryor St: windows from the fourth row up (40-42 is about 15.5m in
  the 2021 photo, 14m in the game).
- Uniform rows: the photos give 3.2m at the bottom to 3.4m at the top, probably the lenses.

## The game's door and the set back ground floor

The doors are about a meter behind the marble, under the overhang, and so are the walls of the
ground floor all along the three streets. The interiors draw their walls, floor and ceiling on
the outline (interiorGeometry.ts), so anything behind it gets dark bands and a line at 3.3m.
build-campus.mjs moves the outline in by a meter on Pryor St, Decatur St and Kimball Way (cm
precision, the corners from osm's nodes) and keeps the south side where osm has it. The ground
floor's walls are drawn on the new outline, the overhang and everything over the soffit on
osm's line. You can walk under the overhang along the walls.

The piers and corner posts stand out on the sidewalk in front of the outline, and the screens
on Pryor St between them: they're circles and walls in game/world.ts (coeObstacles) so you
can't walk through them. The game's door is in the middle of the canopy on Pryor St, 17.9m from
Decatur St, where gsu's 2026 photo has the sliding doors (it was at the far end of Pryor St,
in a bay of screens). The gps route from hurt park goes along Decatur St and is within the test's
limit.

## Cost

8 parts: marble, window glass, dark glass (the strip under the soffit, louvres, vents, the glass
behind the screens), brick, grey metal (screens, the dock door, the roofs), blue (canopies,
banners, the round sign), the soffit, and clear glass at the lobby. The marble, the window
glass and the soffit cast shadows; the window glass has to, the building is empty inside and
the sun came through the rows of windows. The marble is drawn first while you're close. Every
part is one of the library's families with no new options, so it doesn't add any shaders.

About 1.4k triangles (marble 0.6k, glass 0.4k, the rest under 0.1k each).

Measured with the other two buildings from the same day (petit science center, the college of
education and the bookstore went in together): prod builds in one browser, taking turns, hot
plateau on AC, 4 min preheat, 2 rounds thrown away and the median of 4 (ms at 1280x800@2, the
game look, the buildings are the same in both). "before" is the build just before the three (3faae0e). The warm-up builds 798
shaders and 700 pipelines before and after. The 12 old spots all moved -0.83..+0.03 ms (noise),
the college of education's own:

| spot                                                                  | before | now   | now - before |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- | ------------ |
| coe street (decatur st at park place, looking at the pryor st corner) | 10.35  | 10.70 | 0.35         |
| coe close (under the overhang at the doors)                           | 12.13  | 11.93 | -0.20        |

8 parts is more than the others, but the building is out at five points, away from every other
hand-built building, and close up the flat marble and the dark soffit are cheaper than the
generic facade's windows were.
