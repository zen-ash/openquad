Center West) from. Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the
measurements are my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Plan**: osm's outline is right: a box 24.4m along unity plaza with the north corner cut
  off (the glass bay), the courtland st side, a notch 4.25m wide by student center west
  with the doors in it, the party wall along student center west, and the clock tower as a
  bump at the back.
- **Height**: 18.0m to the top of the parapet from unity plaza. The courtland st photo's
  camera gives 12.6-12.9m over the sidewalk, which is 4.65-5.15m over the plaza (usgs); the
  2024 photo's proportions in one column agree if courtland st is 5.2m up.
- **Up the walls**: plain stucco at the plaza level, a lower band (3.5-3.92m), the bottom
  row of windows on it (3.95-5.65m), the middle row (7.9-9.45m) with the upper band
  (9.0-9.42m) across its heads, the sign (11.8-12.95m), the top row (14.6-16.0m) and a
  metal coping. The bands are half round, a pinkish brown. Measured on the 2024 photo with
  three heights as anchors (it's a processed wide shot, I don't read heights off its
  projection directly).
- **Windows**: small, dark teal glass that reflects a bit, thin dark frames, set in 0.3m.
  On the plaza side in columns at 2.2, 4.9, 7.6, 11.9, 14.5, 20.5 and 23m from the north
  corner.
- **Glass bay**: on the north corner, 7.9-16m, six columns of dark bronze mullions, a
  transom at 11.7m. The top two rows have gsu's blue film with the logo and the name; it's
  drawn plain blue (the logo is a trademark). The lower panes are glass that reflects the
  light buildings across the plaza (0.57 as bright as the wall next to it in the 2024
  photo). The upper band runs across in front of it.
- **The notch**: sliding doors between salmon jambs, a round moulding at the door head, a
  round salmon column in the corner, a dark curtain wall up to 15.3m, then a salmon gable
  stepping up to 19.05m with a louvre in it. Its side is salmon under the lower band.
- **Clock tower**: salmon stucco to the eave (20.8m), a light grey standing seam gable roof
  over its southeast 4.5m, a clock (light ring, dark dial) on the plaza side and on the
  end, a slit window under the end's clock.
- **Sign**: "UNIVERSITY BOOKSTORE" on a white plate with a blue edge, 6 by 1.15m, a blue
  square where gsu's logo is.
- **Colors**: compared to things already matched, in the shade. The stucco is matched
  channel by channel to student center east's white block in the 2024 plaza photo (the
  plaza side is what people see most): a light greige, and the overcast 2019 gilmer st
  photo agrees. Commons' 2019 courtland st photo and the 2026 ones under the bridge have
  it warmer, a tan. The salmon is 0.71-0.76 as bright and pinker. The glass in the game
  reads 0.63 of the wall on the bay and 0.48 on the windows (photo 0.57 and 0.33).
- **Roof**: flat, light grey. A glass gable skylight, a light glazed gable, a dark glass
  pyramid and a long light screen behind the plaza side's parapet (it shows over the
  parapet from gilmer st), all by eye.

## Not verified

- The whole southeast side, toward urban life: no photo. It has the plaza side's rows and
  first three columns, and a pair of glass doors by the tower (gsu says there's a door
  toward urban life).
- The courtland side except its last 2m: the top row window by the notch is in the 2019
  photo, the one at the other end is a guess. The 2015 photo has a two line sign there over
  the band; it's not drawn (nothing newer shows it).
- The gable's left half (hidden in every photo, it's the right half mirrored).
- The clock tower's height: mapillary's 2019 photo from gilmer st sees 1.2m of its wall
  over the parapet (eave about 21-21.5m), the aerial looks more like 19.5-20.5m. I used
  20.8. The roof covers about 60% of the top in the aerial and more like 85% from gilmer
  st, I used 4.5 of 5.9m.
- The things on the roof: sizes and heights by eye.
- The notch's curtain wall is near black in commons' 2019 photo (in shade, nothing light
  across from it); in the game it's a dark blue grey, it's the same glass as the bay.
- What's under the courtland st bridge. The 2026 photos show the notch's salmon side, a
  sand wall with a mural going off under the bridge, and the column; the rest is my guess.

## The ground level

Courtland st is a bridge 5.2m over unity plaza and the game's ground is flat. Student
center west is drawn from the courtland st sidewalk, student center east from the plaza.
I drew the bookstore from the plaza, because the doors, the plaza side, the glass bay and
the tower are what people see most, and the doors in the notch really are at the plaza
level (under the bridge). So:

- From courtland st the bookstore is 5.2m too tall. Commons' 2019 photo lines up when the
  game's camera goes 5.2m up (`bs-courtland-up`), not at the photo's own height.
- The courtland side's lower 5.2m is a real wall that's under the bridge deck. It's plain
  stucco with the lower band and a salmon bit by the notch, like the 2026 photos.
- Behind, the party wall stands about 5m over student center west's roof (really they're
  about level). It's plain stucco with a coping, only as high as the gable's ends (17.1m):
  in the 2019 photo nothing of the bookstore shows over them, so the parapet there is lower
  than on the other sides.
- Inside, the party wall's inside walls are 5cm in from the outline: student center west's
  marble faces into the bookstore on that same line and they flickered (it did with the
  generic box too).

In the middle of the notch's back wall, in the sliding doors, facing courtland st

## Cost

5 parts (stucco, salmon, glass, blue, metal), all from the library's families (precast,
glass, metal), so no new shaders. The glass and the blue film don't cast shadows; there's a
plain stucco back behind every window and the bay instead, or the sun shone through the
holes onto the plaza. The stucco is drawn first while you're close (`first`), like the
other big walls. The sign is a regular plate sign (`height` is new on signs, the plate
would be too thin otherwise).

All the dark glass is one material. The windows and the notch's glass are set in a wall
or a notch, so they don't see the bright sky near the horizon the way the bay does: their
normals lean up a bit (toward the darker sky overhead) instead of a second glass material.
Without that they came out lighter than the bay, the other way round from the photos.

About 1.4k triangles (stucco 544, metal 393, salmon 295, glass 196, blue 4).

Measured with the other two buildings from the same day (petit science center, the college of
education and the bookstore went in together): prod builds in one browser, taking turns, hot
plateau on AC, 4 min preheat, 2 rounds thrown away and the median of 4 (ms at 1280x800@2, the
game look, the buildings are the same in both). "before" is the build just before the three (3faae0e). The warm-up builds 798
shaders and 700 pipelines before and after. The 12 old spots all moved -0.83..+0.03 ms (noise),
the bookstore's own:

| spot                                   | before | now   | now - before |
| -------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- | ------------ |
| bs plaza (unity plaza, the plaza face) | 8.78   | 9.70  | 0.93         |
| bs notch (courtland st, at the doors)  | 12.35  | 12.40 | 0.00         |
| scw view                               | 11.03  | 11.15 | -0.10        |
| scw grilles                            | 12.75  | 12.80 | 0.00         |

From the plaza the gpu passes only add up to +0.33 ms (prepass +0.18, scene +0.13); the rest is
