# Classroom South: references

from. Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the measurements are
my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Floors and height**: osm says 6 floors, gsu lists a lobby and six floors. At the Central
  Ave end: a band of low windows along the ground (1.2-1.65m up) under a marble band, five
  rows of small windows 2.5m apart (sills 3.4 to 13.4m), a thin ledge under each row, a top
  row 3.6m higher (1.4 times the others, both the 2019 and the 2025 photos) and the parapet
  at 20.2m. campus.json had 21m (6 x 3.5). The rows are closer together than a floor would
  be; that's what the photos show, I didn't find out why.
- **The street**: Decatur St climbs about 4.5m toward Library South. There the doors are at
  the sidewalk, the lowest two rows are under it and the parapet is about 16m up (the 2025
  photo sees three rows and the top one over the doors). The game's ground is flat, so the
  whole front is drawn like the Central Ave end.
- **Plan**: a slab 61m along Decatur St, 21m deep, deeper (30m) at the Library South end, a
  lower block behind it, and the west wing along Central Ave (osm's outline). The lobby of
  2020 fills the corner between the slab and the wing: osm draws it as part of the wing,
  build-campus.mjs moves it over.
- **Decatur St front**: white georgia marble, grey veined, two slabs to a bay and two to a
  row; a small square window (0.85m) to each bay, bays 2.7m, the first 3.7m from Central Ave.
  The low windows on the ground are set in under the band with a pier at each bay; they stop
  6m short of the doors toward Library South (plain marble behind a brick planter there,
  2021).
- **Windows**: teal tinted glass that shows the sky in the sun (2021, 2025) and reads dark
  from the shade (2019), light frames, set 0.2m into the marble.
- **Ends**: blank marble, big slabs in a running bond. At Library South, doors set in under
  the marble: narrow upright marble boards with the name, a glass door and sidelight under a
  transom, a warm grey stucco with "95 DECATUR ST." (2026).
- **The doors toward Library South**: a cream box round them with wide piers, the name on
  the band over the doors, four glass doors under a transom well back (2021).
- **The lobby (2020)**: glass the whole way (dark bronze frames): a lower glass stair next to
  the slab, a cream box over the doors with a blue stripe and the logo up top (a plain blue
  square here, trademark), the name plate, a dark canopy on two posts, a revolving door and a
  door beside it, then a strip of glass and a tan box on the corner that wraps round onto
  Central Ave.
- **The west wing**: bands of light, grey and tan panels about a meter high in long pieces,
  glass slots up the stairs on Central Ave and at its corner, 14.5m to the top (2019 photo
  from Wall St). Its back and the side toward G Deck are plain light panels (unseen).
- **Roof**: flat, a stair house and a few low units (satellite).
- **Signage**: white plates with a blue edge, the GSU logo (blue square here) and "CLASSROOM
  SOUTH" in dark grey letters, over the lobby doors, over the doors toward Library South,
  and at the end doors with "95 DECATUR ST.".

## Not verified

- The back of the slab, the lower block behind it (its height is a guess, 15m) and the
  wing's back and its side toward G Deck: no photo sees them. Drawn with the front's marble
  and windows (the lower block) and plain light panels (the wing).
- The windows near Library South may step at a stair (the 2021 photo is too close to tell);
  drawn as a regular grid.
- The lobby's top (20.2m, from the 2019 photo from Central Ave) and the glass stair's (17m).
- The cameras for the 2021, 2025 and 2026 photos are by eye: the 2021/2026 ones are too close
  and the 2025 one is from the library plaza, which is higher than the street.

## Cost

8 parts (marble, glass, frames, lobby glass, cream, tan, grey, blue), 6.4k triangles, no
new shaders: the warm-up still builds 877 shaders and 790 pipelines. The window glass and
the blue bits don't cast shadows. The marble is drawn before everything else while you're
within 20m of it (`first`).

Prod builds in one browser, taking turns (each build in each place in turn), 2 rounds thrown
away and the median of 4 (ms at 1920x1200). "before" is the build from before the library
(546ae05), "pre" the one just before Classroom South (effa32b), "now" this. The laptop was
mostly on battery and hot (the charger kept dropping out), so there are two runs: the first
(cooler, build e5c7f77, the same parts and shaders as now) and the last one, on AC for all
but the end (a7893f3):

A marble wall filling the screen (cs close) costs 1-2 ms more than the old generic
building, like Student Center West's marble. Library north and langdale street go from
0-0.3 ms in the first run to 1-1.35 ms in the last; toggling Classroom South in the page
(hide it, or its shadows) gave 1.0 ms at library north once and nothing the next time, so
it's somewhere in there: it's in view behind Library North there, and each part is a draw
call in five passes.
