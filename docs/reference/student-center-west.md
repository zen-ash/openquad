## Facade breakdown

- **Material**: white Georgia marble, grey veined and streaked. Rows of tall slabs (1.7 m,
  about 0.85 m wide) with a row of short wide slabs (0.82 m) between them, stacked straight
  up, no bond. Thin joints. The carved and cast pieces (grilles, columns, copings) are a
  shade darker. The side on the Urban Life plaza is smooth cream panels, not marble.
- **Height and floors**: at the bookstore end 5 tall and 4 short rows above what shows of
  the bottom short one: 12.2 m. Courtland St is a bridge over Decatur St and climbs toward
  it, so at the Decatur St corner only about 10 m shows (the game's ground is flat, it's
  12.2 all round). The old campus.json height was 13 m, a guess from "about 3 floors"; the
  photos say 12.2. No floor lines outside. GSU's plans have 4 levels: the lowest opens onto
  Decatur St under the bridge, so from Courtland St it's 3 floors of about 4 m. A block
  3.7 m higher sits on the roof, set back 5.5 m from Courtland St, from 16 to 55.6 m along
  it (fit to the 2019 corner photo; on the 2023 satellite its roof shows about 6 m further
  toward the bookstore, I went with the photo).
- **Courtland St** (78 m): almost blind. A side door with a small square window over it at
  the Decatur St end (it was Cinefest's), a band of five cast stone grilles (6.6 by 2.05 m,
  2.35 m up, 8.35 m apart) in frames that stick out 0.25 m, with a lattice of bars, little
  blocks with holes (the holes are left out, a few cm across) and five big blocks
  (square, octagon, square, octagon, square), dark windows behind. Then the main entrance 6.5 m from the bookstore end: a 3.5 by 5.1 m
  recess, glass doors in dark bronze, a bronze panel of square ornaments over them, a
  three pane transom. "66" to the left, a small window above, the name plate to the right.
  Six blue GSU banners hang from arms along the top.
- **Decatur St** (34 m): seven carved columns 3.5 m apart, set back into the wall. Each
  goes block, slot window, block, slot window... down from the top: a square block with a
  smaller square on it between rows of little blocks, then a 0.2 by 1.24 m slot window in
  two frames. Only the top four rows show over the bridge railing; the bottom one is made
  up to match (unverified).
- **Urban Life plaza side**: the marble comes round the corner for 3.4 m, then cream
  panels: a long window in light frames, a louver under it, a door recess with the name
  plate over it, low dark brick walls either side. Positions by eye from the one photo.
- **Bookstore end**: the marble returns a few meters where it steps back from the
  bookstore, with a brown panel at the bottom.
- **Roof**: flat, gravel, rooftop units where the satellite shows them.
- **Signage**: aluminium name plates (GSU logo left, a plain blue square here: trademark)
  with dark grey letters, "66" in black.

## Not verified

- The back and the southeast side toward the dock: no photos, drawn as plain cream panels
  like the plaza side.
- The bottom row of the Decatur St columns (hidden by the bridge railing in the photo).
- Positions along the plaza side: from one photo, the camera fit to that side can't check
  them.
- The lower level under the Courtland St bridge (two GSU photos from 2025-2026) isn't
  drawn: the game's ground is flat and Courtland St is at street level in it.
- The "cinéfest" sign over the side door is in the 2019 photo. Cinefest closed, and no
  newer photo shows that door, so I left the sign off.

## Cost

Measured in one browser, going back and forth between a dev server at the commit before
and this one, frame times at 1920x1200 (median of 4-6 rounds each, the laptop was busy so
the absolute numbers are higher than usual):

| view                                              | before | marble() walls | precast() walls | metal() walls |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| the new visual/frametime view                     | 11.2   | 12.5           | 11.8            | 11.1          |
| close up of a grille, the wall filling the screen | 12.5   | 15.8           | 14.8            | 13.6          |

The walls are precast(): marble()'s veins cost a millisecond more when the wall fills the
screen and hardly show from the street, and metal() has no normal or roughness maps. The
first version had 11 materials, now 8 (every material is a draw call in 5 passes).
