# 191 Peachtree Tower: references

What I built 191 Peachtree Tower from: two 50 floor towers of pale granite (1990, Johnson/Burgee)
joined by a link, every outer corner notched, on a base that fills the corners out to a cornice,
with an atrium on Peachtree St and an open temple crown on each tower. It isn't GSU's. It's
about 545m north of Hurt Park, and the crowns are what you see over the rooftops from all over
campus. Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the measurements are my
own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

Heights are elevations (m NAVD88). It's drawn from 319.3m, Peachtree Center Ave at the tower.

- **Plan**: two towers joined by a link, every outer corner notched: 20 points on the lidar's
  roof lines, 76.7 x 36.8m round (-123.65, -530.8). Each tower's north and south faces are
  25.75m, its end face 27.6m, the notches 5.95 x 4.6m, the link 13.3m wide and 4.6m back.
- **Base**: the rectangle round both towers (the notches filled) up to 365, with a cornice 0.6m
  out. Its top three floors have the strips and punched windows. Under a band at 343.5 the lower
  part has tall windows, on the south and east faces only (the west one is inside the atrium,
  the north one never seen).
- **Atrium**: on Peachtree St, x -190..-162, z -559..-509, to 356 at its sides with a ridge
  along it at 361.5 (z -541..-526, lidar), a big window in its front.
- **The low strip** along Peachtree Center Ave (x -85.3..-81.4) to 329.3, a storefront in it.
- **Shaft**: 50 floors, 4.08m apart from Peachtree Center Ave to the parapet at 523.5. Each north,
  south and end face has a column of punched windows 2.7m in from each edge and four glass strips
  2.5m wide, 4.2m apart, in the middle, broken by a granite band every third floor (at floors 49,
  46, 43...). The wings in the notches have one punched column, the link three, the notches'
  short sides none. The strips stop under a frieze at 522.
- **Upper stage**: on each tower, as wide as its north and south faces and as deep as its end
  face, 523.5-532 with a cornice, square windows over the strips, and a dome on a pedestal near
  each corner (to 535.8).
- **Crowns**: on x -143.55 and -103.75: a podium 17m square to 537.5, four corner piers (the
  finials 13.3m apart, lidar) and two slender ones on each side up to 546.5, the frieze and
  cornice to 549, a low balustrade, and a tall pedestal with a ball on each corner to 552.3
  (lidar 552.4). Open in the middle.
- **Colors** (LeMay's 09:53 photo): a pale warm granite, beige, a bit lighter and pinker than
  Southern Bell's limestone in the same light. The glass a dark blue grey.
- **At night** the crowns are lit from their feet (warm, like the real floodlights).

### What I changed from the research notes

- The upper stage is as wide as each tower's north and south faces and as deep as its end face
  (the lidar has 531-532 there), not 4m in from every face.
- The domes are near the stage's corners (11 and 12.2m from its middle) with their tops at the
  lidar's 535.x. Where the notes had them, 4-5m in, the stage's cornice hid them from below.
- The crown: the podium to 537.5, the cornice down at 549, then a balustrade and tall corner
  pedestals with the balls, from the photo. The lidar's ring at 551-552 is the balustrade and the
  pedestals. The notes had the cornice at 549.5-551.5.
- The punched windows are drawn 1.7 x 2.4m (1.5 x 2.2m for real). There are no reveals, and the
  bigger glass stands in for their shadows, which is what you see from campus.
- No mullions in the glass: 300-600m away they'd be finer than a pixel and made the strips pale.
- The outline is the lidar's base, atrium and low strip (8 points), in place of osm's, which had
  the low bits round the block in it and went up 235m as one slab. osm's north strip (z
  -559..-550) is gone.
- The north faces have no windows: all of campus is south of the tower, they always face away.

## Not verified

- The plan is from the roof-top lidar. The shaft's walls under the top aren't measured (the
  photos show no setbacks between the base and the parapet).
- The floor pitch assumes 50 equal floors from Peachtree Center Ave.
- The base is from one photo: its arched windows, oculi and the big arched entrance are drawn as
  tall rectangles, the atrium's columned front on Peachtree St isn't drawn.
- The upper stage's windows (how many, the arches) and the crown's details (the piers' sizes,
  the frieze, the balustrade) are from photos 300-560m away.
- The link's faces (3 columns) are from a part of the photo in shadow.
- The colors are from one sunny morning series.

## The ground level

Drawn from 319.3m, Peachtree Center Ave at the tower. The block rises about 9.5m to Peachtree St
(328.8m): the atrium's front stands on that higher ground and its walls go down into it.

## campus.json

## Cost

3 parts: granite (precast), glass, and crown (the brick family, for its uplights at night: from
campus the bricks are far too small to see). No new shaders or textures. About 5.1k triangles:
granite 2.55k, glass 1.66k, crown 0.92k. The glass sits flush in its holes and doesn't cast
shadows. It's in view from almost everywhere on campus.

Measured after the merge.
