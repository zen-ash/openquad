West) from. Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the measurements
are my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Plan**: osm's outline is right: a slab 54.1m by 27.5m with round brick towers (6.26m
  across) on its corners and halfway along the long sides, all standing half out of the
  walls, and a lower wing with two more towers toward student center west. The tower
  centers come from osm's circles (they fit its nodes to a few cm) and line up with the
  tower tops on the satellite. The tower halfway along the plaza side isn't in osm (the bit
  joining the slab to 140 decatur st is there instead); the photos and the satellite have it.
  build-campus.mjs redraws the outline from the tower centers.
- **Height**: 12 floors in osm is right, the street floor and 11 more ("11th floor" is on
  gsu's map), but they're tall floors. From piedmont ave it's 50.8m to the top of the
  parapet: the courtland st photo's camera says 50.2 (fitted on four towers, the camera on
  the bridge 10m over the tower's base going by usgs), overture's lidar says 50.6 (49m over
  the ground on the northeast side, 1.6m higher than piedmont ave), the piedmont ave
  photo's camera 52.1 (it depends on one pixel for the tower's base).
- **Up the slab**: a tall street floor (5.4m), three floors of brick, a ledge that sticks
  out all round (17.3-18.5m), a floor of dark glass set back behind the piers (with
  louvres), then seven floors 3.75m apart and a 2.9m parapet. Measured on the fitted
  piedmont ave photo (the bands are clear in the sun), scaled to the 50.8m.
- **Bays**: between two towers, three bays with a cream pier either side, a narrow slit of
  dark glass next to every pier and a strip of glass next to each tower; grey brown brick
  in between, a cream band under every row of brick. The piers and bands stand out 0.3m.
- **Towers**: plain brick the color of the panels, a bit higher than the parapet (the 2025
  photos).
- **Letters**: "GEORGIA STATE UNIVERSITY" on the parapet at both ends, "GSU" on the long
  sides near the north and the south towers, dark bronze.
- **Colors**: in gsu's 2025 photo in the sun the brick is 0.69 as bright as the bands and a
  pinkish grey (r > b > g); the bands and piers a warm off white.
- **The wing**: it stands on the plaza, a floor up from piedmont ave: storefront behind round
  cream columns up to the band with "URBAN LIFE CENTER" on it, then two strips of windows
  between cream spandrels, the slab's own rows (gsu's 2026 photos have the storefront and two
  strips under the top band). Its top is level with the slab's ledge (2010; lidar 326.3m,
  18.9m over piedmont ave). The plate is drawn with the blue square where GSU's logo is.
- **140 decatur st**: a brick box with a deep cream band round the top (2010), its roof at
  323.1m (lidar, 15.7m over piedmont ave), standing on decatur st. Drawn with urban life,
  with the bit joining it to the slab.
- **The deck by piedmont ave**: between 140 decatur st, the slab and the corner of piedmont
  ave and decatur st, pavers a floor up (lidar 313.3m) over brick on a white base with a
  solid precast parapet, and a short brick tower on the corner (lidar 318m). The slab's side
  over it has a storefront at the deck's level under its first band (2010).

## Not verified

- The deck: its outline is from the lidar (pavers at 313.3m between x 0..22, z 250..282 in
  game meters) and two photos, the short tower's place on its corner is good to 2-3m (the
  2019 photo from piedmont ave and the 2010 one put it a few meters apart). The openings in
  its brick on decatur st (2010) and the wall lights are left out. It's solid: you can't
  get up onto it (in real life it's reached from the plaza and by stairs from decatur st).
- The lidar has the roof at 357.7m and the towers' tops at 358.8-358.9m, the game has the
  parapet at 358.2m and the towers at 359.7m. Within a meter, left as the photos had it.
- The wing's side toward student center east and its end toward student center west: made
  like the plaza side (not in any photo). The real plaza side steps back at its east end.
- The side toward student center east below the ledge: behind the generic box osm has there
  (way 801359973 area, a loading dock in gsu's dock photo).
- The boxes on the roof: sizes by eye from the satellite.
- Where the glass floor's louvres are and what's behind them: drawn as dark glass.

## The game's door and the passage

The doors under "URBAN LIFE CENTER" open into a walk through the middle of the wing, out to
the steps up to unity plaza by student center east. Osm has it as a footway (way
780276694, tagged as a tunnel because the plaza is a floor under unity plaza) and the plaza
as a pedestrian area (780276691, layer -1); build-campus used to drop both, so the plaza
wasn't paved and nothing walked onto it. Now:

- build-campus keeps the footway and the plaza area, and adds a walk across unity plaza (an
  area in osm, the game only walks along lines) from the top of the steps, between the
  bookstore and student center east, to the footway along its north side.
- The passage is open in the game, in the wing's middle bay (6.1m wide, under the strip
  windows), with storefront along both sides. The wing past it is its own building in
  campus.json (gsu, a part, drawn with urban life).
- The game's door is in the passage's side toward the slab, 3.5m in from the plaza, right
  behind the name. The gps route from hurt park goes across unity plaza and down the steps:
  310m (it was 532m round by decatur st, over the 478m the test allows).

In real life the plaza end has glass doors and the walk through is a lobby, not open air.

## The ground

Piedmont ave at the south tower is 307.4m and the plaza with the doors a floor up: lidar has
its pavers at 313.3m, and the deck by piedmont ave at the same height. Decatur st runs from
307.2m at piedmont ave up to 309-310m, the side toward student center east is at 308.5-310m.

The building is drawn from piedmont ave (`DATUMS` in game/surface.ts, `{ from: 307.4 }`), so
the parapet is at 358.2m and the wing's top at 326.3m wherever the plaza is. The floor inside
is the game's ground at the door (311.68m), and everything at the plaza's level is drawn
from there: the wing's storefront, the passage and its door, the storefront on the slab's
side toward the plaza. The game's terrain is usgs bare earth, which runs under the plaza's
deck, so the game's plaza is 1.6m lower than the real one (311.7 against 313.3m) and a
rounded hump instead of a flat deck. That makes the storefronts on the plaza 4.9m tall
instead of 3.3m; if the terrain gets the deck one day, they come out right by themselves.

Toward piedmont ave and student center east the slab has its tall street floor at the
street. On the plaza side its first floor up is a storefront at the plaza's level, with the
plaza's edge (plain precast) under it where the game's ground is lower. Every wall that
stands on the ground goes on down to the lowest ground under the building
(campus/landmark.ts `sink`), so there's no plinth.

Before this the whole building stood on the game's ground at the door: 4.3m too tall, with
140 decatur st floating over decatur st and a plain plinth under the slab.

## Cost

3 parts (precast, brick, glass), all casting shadows: the building is empty inside, and with
the glass left out the sun came through the rows of windows onto the ground behind it.
Everything is one of the library's families, so no new shaders: the warm-up builds 798
shaders and 700 pipelines before and after.

About 10.2k triangles (brick 5.9k, precast 3.3k, glass 1k). Prod builds in one browser,
taking turns, hot plateau on AC, 4 min preheat, 2 rounds thrown away and the median of 4
(ms at 1280x800@2, the game look). "before" is the build just before this (c7086a1):
