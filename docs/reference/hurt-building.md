# Hurt Building: references

What I built the Hurt Building (45 Edgewood Ave / 50 Hurt Plaza SE, 1913-1926, J.E.R.
Carpenter) from: the tower across Peachtree Center Ave from Hurt Park, where everyone spawns. It
isn't GSU's, so it has no door and no inside. Everything here is reference only: no photos are in
the repo, the measurements are my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

Heights are over 311.3m, the Peachtree Center Ave sidewalk at the chamfer (see the ground level).

- **Plan**: osm's outline is the whole triangle. It's really two wings, on Edgewood Ave and on
  Hurt Plaza (30.6 degrees apart), meeting at the apex in the west, with a light court between
  them over the base on Peachtree Center Ave. The base's outline is the lidar's roof edges less
  the cornice (0.85m: the lidar's ground comes to 0.25m of that on Edgewood Ave): Edgewood Ave
  88.9m, a 7m chamfer at Peachtree Center Ave, the edgewood wing's end 15.8m, the base across
  the court (1.1m back from the wing) 21m, the hurt plaza wing's end 18.2m, Hurt Plaza 73.7m,
  the apex 14.5m (square to the wings' bisector). The court is 32m deep, 6m wide at the back.
- **Floors**: three tiers of base, an attic, 13 floors of shaft (the top one terra cotta), the
  cornice. Counted up the chamfer (2013, 2017) and the apex (2020). By cross ratio on the sills
  (13 in a row, 3px), the band under the shaft is 4.7 floors over the sidewalk and the crown
  1.9 over the top floor's panel. With the lidar's crown (381.4m) a floor is 3.75m. Base tiers
  2.4-5.7, 6.9-9.4, 10.7-12.8, the entablature to 15.4, attic windows 15.7-16.9, band
  17.2-17.5, the top window's head 66.25, the crown 70.1 (381.4m), the roof 68.7 (380m).
- **The shaft**: very pale grey glazed brick (its courses faint), the windows in tall recesses
  (0.3m to the glass) between piers about half a pair wide (the 2017 photo's ratios): a pair
  (two 1.5m windows, 0.14m of brick between, nearly touching) or a single. Under each window a
  cream terra cotta panel 1.1m tall, a bit proud of the glass: rails top and bottom, a sunk
  field per window with a rosette in it, a sill along its top. The window over it 2.65m,
  1-over-1, white frames, pale glass that shows the sky. Edgewood Ave a single, 16 pairs, a
  single; the chamfer one single; the edgewood wing's end 3 pairs; the hurt plaza wing's end
  single, 2 pairs, single; Hurt Plaza single, 13 pairs, single; the apex single, pair, single.
- **The top**: the top floor all terra cotta, a tall raised panel on each pier with a roundel at
  its head (gold in real life), a medallion on the frieze over each pier, dentils, a corbel
  every meter (sloped foot, 1m out) under the crown 1.15m out, a dark lip. The cornice goes round
  the outside and stops at the court.
- **The base**: white Georgia marble with grey and tan veins, an opening under each pair 0.55m
  deep: three tiers of glass in dark frames (three lights, a transom in the taller tiers), cast
  iron panels between them (three sunk fields with a boss in each, proud of the glass), solid
  corner piers. The entablature: architrave, frieze, dentils, the cornice 0.6m out and its
  moulding. Then the terra cotta attic with a small window under each one above and a
  cartouche on each pier. The chamfer has a tall window (a cast iron panel across it), a round
  window and a half round hood over the arched door (Starbucks).
- **The base across the court**: three wide openings (Ionic pilasters between in real life),
  the attic, a brick parapet to 19.6 (330.9m). Behind it the base's roof (329m) for 12.5m, then
  the court's floor at 10.4 (321.7m). The court's walls are plain brick, a window to each floor;
  dark glass up the back (2013 photo).
- **The rotunda**: on the apex, its middle 3.9m out. Esri's newest image has its balustrade
  about 5m round (the lidar's 6.2m is the cornice plus its 0.5m cells). A colonnade: seven
  columns 4.95m round (the two at the ends half in the apex, about 47 degrees apart, the 2020
  photo has four over its front half) on pedestals, with capitals; tall windows between them
  (a door's height, a transom, glass up to under the capitals), a strip of marble behind each
  column. The entablature to 14.3, the cornice 5.85m round to 15.05, a balustrade (plinth,
  balusters every 0.33m, a post over each column, the rail) to 17.3 (328.6m).
- **The penthouse**: brick, 6.7m over the roof (386.7m) where the wings meet, a machine room on
  it to 77.4 (388.7m, the json's height).
- **Colors** (sun 2017, overcast 2020, ratios inside each photo): the brick nearly white in sun,
  the terra cotta 0.85-0.9 of it and warmer, the marble like the brick, the cast iron near black.
- **Sign**: "HURT BUILDING" in white on a red plate on the apex by the rotunda (2020).

### What I changed from the first measurements

- The floors: a camera fit to the lidar's corners put the band a floor lower (3.9m floors), and
  the render at that camera had the base too short against the photo. The cross ratio rulers
  don't need a camera and agree with each other, the floors are from them.
- Round 2: the windows bigger and the pair's mullion thinner (1.5m and 0.14m from 1.37m and
  0.3m), so the piers come to about half a pair like in the photos; the panels, cast iron,
  entablature, corbels and the rotunda got their relief; the rotunda is smaller (esri), a
  colonnade instead of a drum; the glass paler.

## Not verified

- The Edgewood Ave face: too foreshortened to count in every photo, the 16 pairs are the Hurt
  Plaza module over its length.
- The floor height is 3.75m +-0.1: the crown's projection toward a camera looking up makes the
  top end of the rulers uncertain.
- The rotunda's size and how far it's out (3.9m): esri's ring and the lidar's disc disagree by
  about a meter. I couldn't fit a camera to the 2020 photo of the apex: it's a heavily
  processed phone crop, and there's no second photo of that corner to fit it with (the 2022
  mapillary phones point at the sidewalk), so it's compared from about the same direction only.
- The court: its walls' windows and the base's roof are by eye, only the lidar sees inside it.
  Its windows are flat (no reveals), you only see them from up high.
- Not drawn: the fine ornament (the gold color of the top panels, the shields on the cast iron,
  the acanthus of the capitals, the little arches between the corbels), the Ionic pilasters
  across the court's base, the orange H on Edgewood Ave, the court's lower roofs (317.8m,
  325.4m).

## The ground level

The street climbs 4.7m from the chamfer (311.3m) to the apex (316.0m). The base's floors stay
level and the sidewalk cuts across them: it's drawn from 311.3m (DATUMS in game/surface.ts, the
lowest round it), and each opening of the base starts 0.5m over the ground under it, so on the
west end the first tier's under the street like for real. The rotunda's columns stand on the
ground at the apex.

## The side by side

The 2017 photo from Hurt Park, at its own lens (16mm on an NX1, 71 degrees tall): a camera at
(-80, 4, -10.8), heading 264, pitch 28, rolled 2.3 degrees. The crown, the base's openings on
the wing's end and the chamfer's window line up with the photo, the base comes out a bit big at
the bottom (the real lens bends a little). The game's trees stand where the photographer did,
they're hidden for that shot.

## Cost

6 parts (marble, brick, terra, dark, glass, store), all from the library's families, so no new
shaders and no new textures. About 36.7k triangles, two thirds of them the terra cotta (the
shaft's 585 panels: rails, fields, rosettes as four sided bosses, sills; the corbels; the top's
panels). The bays are one hole each in the brick, faces nobody sees are left out (the rails'
backs, the court's reveals). The glass doesn't cast shadows; a dark box just inside the walls
(and a ring inside the rotunda) does, so the sun can't come in one window and out another. Not
drawn first.
