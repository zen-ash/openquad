# Student Recreation Center: references

What I built the Student Recreation Center (2001, 101 Piedmont Ave, across Piedmont Ave from
Student Center East and Urban Life) from. It's still GSU's rec center today (pool, courts,
climbing wall, fitness floors). Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo,
the measurements are my own. Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Plan**: osm's outline is the right shape but 0.5-1.7m off, misses the low dock annex by the
  N Deck and is solid to the ground where the colonnade and the court under the wing are open.
  build-campus.mjs redraws it in a frame along Piedmont Ave (u toward Gilmer St, v in from
  Piedmont Ave) on the lidar's walls, cm precision. The outline is the ground floor: the back
  of the colonnade, the atrium's glass, the court line under the wing and the wing's set back
  storefront on Gilmer St. Everything over them stands out past it.
- **Height**: 18.6m to the gym box's corners (lidar), its roof 17.9m. The main box on Piedmont
  Ave is 14.2m, the same as Student Center East across the street (they're the same height for
  real).
- **Piedmont Ave**: a dark fascia at 4.2m, the second floor's glass, a 2m band of grey louvres,
  a strip of windows (two panes by two in dark frames, a white strip between, set back 10cm),
  the white coping to 13.2m and a dark standing seam slope up to the roof behind it. Eleven
  louvred boxes stand on top, 7.3m apart. At the Decatur St end the face is plain panels from
  the fascia up (11m), and a narrow plain strip at the notch end.
- **The colonnade**: the whole length of Piedmont Ave, 3.5m deep, round columns under each box
  (two under the plain corner too, in the 2019 photo from Decatur St). The back wall is
  storefront glass with cream panels at both ends, a door and a window at the Decatur St end.
- **The entrance**: the main box's end over the last bay is dark ribbed metal to the top. The
  atrium's glass goes from the storefront and doors up to 17.2m, with eight sawtooth skylights
  facing Piedmont Ave behind it, and a flat canopy over the doors. In front, the louvred band
  (7.5-9.9m) with "GEORGIA STATE UNIVERSITY", the red bar under STATE and "STUDENT RECREATION
  CENTER", on three round columns, with white open frames going back from it at both ends and
  where the wing starts. Low white walls either side of the walk to the doors.
- **The wing on Gilmer St**: dark ribbed metal over an open court (underside 4.9m, top 9.6m),
  a 2 by 3 window toward the doors. On Gilmer St a white band, a ribbon of glass and louvres
  over a storefront set back 1.2m behind a low white wall.
- **The gym box**: white metal panels in long rows. On Gilmer St they stand 0.3m out over a
  cream base, with a dark ribbed recess up the wall by the east corner, which is a bit taller.
  On the back drive: a louvre strip with a glass slot, a louvre box in a white frame, the glass
  bays and the S Deck door under a white canopy, a dark ribbed strip, and by the east corner
  the cream base and a dark ribbed band under the panels.
- **The back toward the N Deck**: cream block with dark ribbed metal over it and a roll up door,
  the low dock annex the same.
- **The Decatur St end**: the main box's end is dark ribbed metal up to a parapet at 15.2m, with
  dark glass over the colonnade's open end and a slot window. The white block with a standing
  seam roof sloping down to the patio, the pool's glass wall (panes about 1.9 by 1.2m) and its
  glass roof up to the main box, and the wing behind it with a glass end toward Decatur St.
- **The patio**: the steel beam on four round columns along Piedmont Ave with light panels hung
  behind it (lidar has it 3.3m deep), the black fence round the patio behind them.
- **The corner sign** at the canopy's Decatur St end: two grey panels in a steel frame, the
  name's on a white pillar and the logo's on a steel post, the frame's leg coming down on the
  canopy's last column. The flame logo is a plain blue square (it's GSU's trademark). The lidar
  has the frame's top 7m over the sidewalk.
- **Colors**: ratios inside the photos. The white panels are much whiter than Student Center
  East's white block across the street, the warm grey precast (the block on the Decatur St end,
  the ends of the colonnade, the bases) 0.78 of them facing the same way, the louvres about half
  of the white (0.5 in the 2013 photo, the game 0.5 with the sun the same way), the dark ribbed
  metal about 0.45. The deep soffits (the colonnade, the court) are grey: the game's ambient
  occlusion only reaches about a meter and they came out white.

### What I changed from the research notes

- The colonnade goes under the corner too. The research had a solid corner block, but the 2019
  photo from Decatur St has two columns under it and the cream back wall with a door.
- At the entrance the research had left and right swapped: the grey box with the green window
  is the wing's side, and the main box's end over the colonnade is dark ribbed metal to the top.
  The 2013 photo's camera solved by hand put the atrium's top and the end's top where the lidar
  has them.
- No canopy across the front of the atrium (the 2013 photo has none, the lidar only has
  something 4-8m high in front of the wing). The band is longer than in the notes (the lidar
  has it from -97 to -63 and the Gilmer St photo has its frame sticking out past the wing).
- The patio canopy and the sign are further back than in the notes: the lidar has the canopy's
  beam on the face's line and the sign's frame at v 209.5-212.3, not on the curb (those points
  were the traffic signal's arm).
- The wing's storefront on Gilmer St is set back 1.2m under it (Gilmer St photo), so the outline
  moved in there.

## Not verified

- Nothing newer than 2019 shows any street side. The band's lettering only reads in the 2013
  photo.
- The colonnade's depth, its back wall, the column size and their line, and the soffit: by eye.
  The two columns under the corner and the one at the notch end are from photos where the
  camera is weak that close.
- The second floor's glass looks tilted in the close photos; it's drawn upright.
- The north corner photo's camera couldn't be fitted, so the band's end frames, the beam in
  front of the wing, the wing's columns and the court's low walls are by eye.
- Gilmer St and the back drive: one fitted photo (weak along the face) and a few by eye. The
  panel layout, the recesses and the louvre boxes are approximate, the dock side is made up from
  two phone photos.
- The corner sign: the panels' size and which way they face are by eye (the lidar only has the
  frame's top).
- The wing on the Decatur St end is only seen far behind the sign in one photo.
- The fence's pickets are drawn a bit wider than real so they don't break up into dots, and in
  the game's light they come out grey where the photo has them black.
- Colors: the street photos are overcast with a grey sky, the game always has a blue one, so
  the glass reflects blue in the game where the photos have it grey.

## The ground level

The site falls about 4m from the Piedmont Ave doors to the back drive, and 1.3-2.3m along
Piedmont Ave toward Decatur St. The game is flat, so I picked the Piedmont Ave sidewalk at the
main doors (308.8m) as the game's ground. The main box's roof then lines up with Student Center
East's across the street, the doors and the court in front of them sit right. What it costs:

- the colonnade's Decatur St end shows 1.3m less column than real, the corner 1.6m less and
  the Decatur St end 2.3m less (the tops are at the right height, everything is drawn down to
  the game's ground),
- Gilmer St loses 1.4-4m of the cream base,
- the back drive is 3.9m lower for real: the S Deck door's porch and glass are drawn from the
  game's ground, so they're 7-8m tall instead of 11,
- the low dock annex is drawn 4.8m over the game's ground and the patio canopy 4.2m over it
  (that's how high they are over their own drive and sidewalk), the corner sign 7m.

## The game's door

campus.json had the door on the blank west corner. It's in the atrium's glass under the sign
band now, where the main doors are (osm's entrance node): [106.84, 213.56], facing Piedmont
Ave. The gps route from Hurt Park gets there along Piedmont Ave (the navgraph test passes) and
the Rec Center spot in the places menu is in the court in front of it. Since it moved about 70m,
the lobby furniture now loads by the doors: furniture.ts already treats the rec center as a gym,
so it's 22 pieces (armchairs, coffee tables, plants) within 16m of the door, seen through the
storefront.

The columns, the corner sign's pillar and post, the patio fence, the low walls in the court and
on Gilmer St are collision in game/world.ts (recObstacles). The colonnade, the court under the
wing and the space under the patio canopy are outside the outline, you walk under them.

## Cost

6 parts (white, cream, louvre, dark, glass, clear), all from the library's families, so no
new shaders and no new textures. The red bar and the logo's blue square are plain plates like
the other signs' plates (NameSign draws a plate with no text now). The glass doesn't cast
shadows: it's in walls with the building behind it, and the clear storefronts only go up to
the inside's ceiling. The white and the cream are drawn first when you're close. The colonnade,
the court under the wing and the wing's overhang on Gilmer St have a ceiling 0.8m up inside for
the shadows only (the shadow lookup is pushed out past a soffit's edge, Part 27).

About 5.1k triangles: dark 2.9k (the patio fence's pickets are 2.3k of it), white 1.3k, glass
0.6k, louvre 0.3k, the rest under 0.1k. The louvres and the ribbed metal are the metal family's
ribs turned across, a quad per face.

Measured on Oct 6 2026: prod builds in one browser, taking turns, on AC, 4 min preheat, 2
rounds thrown away and the median of 4 (1280x800@2, the game look, the buildings are the same
in both). "before" is the build just before this (58afebd). The warm-up builds 798 shaders and
700 pipelines before and after. Other Chrome tabs were open (median 23% cpu), so whole frames
moved up to 1.6 ms either way between builds even where nothing changed; the gpu numbers
(timestamps on every pass, paired by round) are the ones to go by:

| spot                                     | before | now   | gpu now - before |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------ | ----- | ---------------- |
| rec street (piedmont ave, looking up it) | 12.55  | 12.17 | 0.22             |
| rec door (in front of the atrium doors)  | 14.80  | 14.42 | 0.09             |

Every other spot is within 0.26 ms of gpu. It's about as cheap as the generic box it
replaces: flat panels, few parts, and the lobby furniture is the gym kind (22 pieces by the
door). The petit-science visual view and its frame spot stood in what's now the fenced patio
at the corner, so both moved out onto Decatur St.

A 2 minute walk down Piedmont past it was clean (1% and 0.1% low 16.8 ms, no spikes) in 3 of
4 tries; the other had 4 gpu waits, no shader builds, and the same build was clean the next
time.
