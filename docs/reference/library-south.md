Everything here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the measurements are my own.
Photos I downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

- **Floors and height**: nine floors of 5m, a band at every floor line, and the coping at
  45.3m (the 2019 photo from Courtland St fitted on the building's corners, with the
  camera on the bridge 5m over Decatur St: the bands line up with 5m lines). osm says 9
  floors, and campus.json had 31.5m (9 x 3.5): the photos win, 45.3m.
- **The street**: Courtland St is a bridge over Decatur St along the building's east side;
  the Courtland St photos stand on it, about 5m up. The plaza on the other side is at about
  the same height (the 2026 photo's camera came out 5.3m over the street). The game's
  ground is flat.
- **Plan**: osm's outline is right (checked on the satellite): 16.4m on Decatur St, 26.6m
  on Courtland St, a long side toward g deck, two short cut corners, and the side toward
  library north and the plaza, 29.4m. The corner piece osm has between it and classroom
  south is the loading dock with the plaza over it.
- **Brick**: an even buff brick, a little warmer than classroom south's marble and about
  0.87 as bright (2026 photo, both in shade). The street floor and the glass box are a
  cream brick only a little lighter (1.03 in the 2026 photo), with a stripe of the tan one
  every 0.8m: clear in the 2021 dashcam, faint on the 2024 dock photo.
- **Bands**: a stone band at every floor line, barely lighter than the brick, sticking out a
  few cm with a thin lip that throws a shadow line (2016, 2019). A stone coping.
- **Windows**: narrow slots about 0.45m wide, a floor tall, in pairs and on their own, never
  lined up from one floor to the next; dark glass in a light frame, set in the brick.
  Courtland St: 2-8 per floor (rectified from the 2019 photo), and big glass on floors 2
  and 3 at the g deck end. Decatur St: fewer slots, two wider ones by the Courtland St
  corner on floors 2 and 3, and two wide windows with a planter under them on the street
  floor. The side toward g deck (satellite): the west two thirds blank, slots at the east
  end.
- **The glass side (toward library north)**: dark glass the whole height over the plaza
  entrance, all but a strip of brick at the west end. Each floor has tall panes and a row
  of short ones along its top, about 1.5m between mullions, with a dark bar at each floor
  line (2007, 2025). The top floor reads darker.
- **Entrance**: the main doors are on the plaza (the 2nd floor, from the walkways over
  Decatur St): a tall storefront 2.6m back under a soffit at 9.8m, a deep grey fascia in
  two tiers (to 11.4m and 12.3m), two round grey columns in front that go up to a beam over
  the next floor (red in 2007), "LIBRARY SOUTH" in letters on the glass (2026). At street
  level, glass doors under the link and doors under the plaza, each with a white name
  plate (2018). The game's door is the one under the plaza entrance.
- **The plaza**: a deck at 5.2m from classroom south's end to the link, out to the street,
  on round columns along it and a leaning one by the link, light square pavers (2026), the
  loading dock under it. On it, in the corner by classroom south, a low glass box with
  cream brick piers, a dark red tile base (drawn dark grey) and a dark fascia with a lip,
  8.85m to the top (measured on the fitted 2026 photo).
- **Roof**: flat and white, a big screened yard for the fans at the Decatur St end and a
  louvred box toward classroom south (satellite).
- **Signage**: white plates with the name and a blue square where GSU's logo is (the logo
  is a trademark), and "LIBRARY SOUTH" in light letters over the plaza doors.

## Not verified

- The side toward g deck below the fourth floor (in g deck's shadow on every satellite
  image) and the two short cut corners (only a smudge from above): made like the rest,
  pairs and single slots.
- The Decatur St slots above the third floor, away from the Courtland St corner: the 2018
  and 2021 photos see them, but I couldn't fit those cameras, so they're placed by eye.
- The plaza deck's outline: the street edge and the columns are from the 2018 photo, the
  outline by eye from the satellite and osm's walkways. The street floor under it (the
  doors, the dock) from the 2018 and 2024 photos, not measured.
- The glass wall's lower floors behind the link, and the dim room behind the plaza doors.

## Where it meets the link and classroom south

- **The library link** (osm way 301958707, a generic raised box): osm's outline landed on
  the glass side and 5.6m round the corner onto the Decatur St side, over the street
  floor's windows. The 2018 and 2021 photos have no bridge there, so build-campus.mjs drops
  that point of the link's outline and it meets the building at the corner. Nothing else
  of the link changed: it's still 3.5-7m up, though the photos show a glass bridge about
  three floors tall.
- **Classroom South**: nothing in its code changed. Its end doors ("95 DECATUR ST.") are
  now under the plaza deck, which runs along its end wall 4.2-6m up, in front of its
  marble. The deck stops at its front corner.

## Cost

9 parts (brick, cream brick, stone, slot glass, the glass wall, grey metal, dark metal,
see-through glass, pavers), 3.7k triangles with the plaza deck and the corner, no new
shaders: the warm-up still builds 877 shaders and 790 pipelines. The slot glass, the
see-through glass and the pavers don't cast shadows. The glass wall and the dark bars
between its floors do: shadows are drawn from the faces turned away from the sun, and
without them the sun came through the building onto the plaza (in stripes, with only the
bars left out). The brick is drawn before
everything else while you're within 20m of it (`first`).
