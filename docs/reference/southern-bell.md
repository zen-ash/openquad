# Southern Bell Building: references

What I built the Southern Bell Telephone Company Building (51 Peachtree Center Ave NE) from: the
1929 art deco telephone exchange in buff limestone, built up to 14 floors by 1963, with the 1963
microwave shaft on its roof. It's AT&T's downtown exchange today, not GSU's. It stands north of
Edgewood Ave behind the Woodruff Volunteer Center deck, and from the campus edge (Edgewood Ave,
Courtland St) the shaft and its lattice tower are what you recognise, 150-200m away. Everything
here is reference only: no photos are in the repo, the measurements are my own. Photos I
downloaded are kept outside the repo.

## Facade breakdown

Heights are elevations (m NAVD88). The building is drawn from 315.8m, Peachtree Center Ave at
the entrance.

- **Plan**: the lidar's parapet lines, x -55..12.9, z -212.3..-163.2 (67.9 x 49.1m), within
  0.5-0.8m of osm's tower part.
- **Pavilions and bays**: a pavilion at each end of every face goes up to the parapet (383.5m).
  The bay between them (19.9m on the north and south faces, 15.5m on the east and west) steps
  back as it goes up: at the face to 351, 2m in to 364.5, 4m in to 373.9, 6m in to the parapet.
  On the north and south faces the pavilions step back 1m from 369 up, so every corner has a 1m
  step 14.5m under the parapet.
- **Floors**: 14, 4.55m apart, the windows' heads from 380.9 down (fitted on the east face
  photo, each within 0.3m). Windows 1.25 x 2.4m, set 0.3m in. The pavilions have an edge
  window, three in the middle, an edge window (0, 2.1 and 5.6m from the middle on the east and
  west, 0, 2.6 and 7m on the north and south). The east and west bays have 4 columns, the north
  and south bays 8 narrow ones (1m) between fins. The top two floors of the north and south
  pavilions are tall slots between fins. A bit over a third of the openings are buff louvres.
- **Fins**: 0.6m wide, 0.35m out, standing 0.8m over the top: on the north and south
  pavilions' top two floors, and over the top floors of the bay's second and third steps.
- **Penthouse**: x -33..-17, z -199..-172, to 390.
- **The shaft**: bronze, 10.2m square (x -48.35..-38.15, z -178.2..-168), from the roof to 414,
  with white trim up each corner and thin light ribs about every 1.15m. It flares out to the
  rim (13.5 x 13.6m) at 418.5, a white fascia round the rim to 420. 12 grey horn antennas on
  the rim (420-423.8) and the white lattice tower (3.6 x 4.2m) to 443, a dish near its top on
  the north east corner.
- **Colors** (LeMay's 10:12 photo, ratios inside it): the limestone a greyish buff, the shaft's
  bronze about 7.7 times darker (an olive grey in the sun), the trim and the lattice lighter
  than the stone, the louvres a buff grey. From campus the windows read near black.

### What I changed from the research notes

- The third step is at 373.9, not 374: the windows of the floor over it start at 373.95.
- The ledge on the north and south faces is at 369 (the floor over it starts at 369.4).
- The lattice is 3.6 x 4.2m, not the lidar's 4.7 x 5.4: that box has the dish and antennas in
  it, and the lattice is narrower than that in both fitted photos.
- The horns go to 423.8, not 428: from campus they're about 3.5m tall on the rim. The lidar's
  426-428 is probably the lattice's foot or the antennas on it.
- No AT&T globe (trademark), not even a plain disc: the bronze is plain there.
- The windows' white sash frames aren't drawn. From 150-200m away they came out a pixel wide
  round every window and made the windows pale, the photos' windows read dark.
- The north face has no windows: all of campus is south of it (the fence's north edge is about
  z -40), so it always faces away. The west face's windows have no reveals, it's only seen at a
  slant from Peachtree Center Ave.

## Not verified

- The north and west faces aren't in any photo I used, they're drawn like the east and south.
- The north and south pavilions' window columns are from one photo at a slant (5 columns, a 6th
  near the corner is possible).
- Which openings are louvres is random, not window by window.
- The horns: 12 here, 8-12 in the photos. They're square boxes flaring up and out, the real
  ones are horn reflectors.
- The shaft's body (10.2m) is from the fitted photo, the lidar only sees its rim.
- The fins' tops are rounded or stepped for real, flat here. The bay's outer steps are about 1m
  wider each side than the inner ones in the photos, here they're all the bay's width.
- The wing east of the tower (the rest of osm's block outline) is a plain 12m box in the regular
  buildings mesh. For real it's 5-18m with cooling towers on it. It's hardly seen from campus.

## The ground level

The block falls about 5m from Peachtree Center Ave (316.2m at the north west corner, 315.8m at
the entrance) to its south east corner on Auburn Ave (310.6m). It's drawn from 315.8m (DATUMS in
game/surface.ts), and its walls carry on down past the lowest ground round it.

## campus.json

There were two "AT&T" buildings: 155, osm's whole block outline (way 270880880) as a 15m
podium, and 310, the tower part (way 886352432) on the end of the list. build-campus.mjs
SOUTHERN_BELL: 310 is "Southern Bell Building" now, on the lidar's rectangle, 67.7m (the parapet
over the datum), `landmark: {}`. 155 keeps its name and index but is cut down in place to the
low wing east of the tower (12m), so its box doesn't stand round the tower's walls any more.
Nothing else changed. It isn't GSU's: no door, no inside, no label.

## Cost

5 parts (stone, glass, bronze, light, grey) from the library's families (precast, glass, metal),
so no new shaders and no new textures. About 6.9k triangles: stone 5.0k (the reveals and fins),
glass 0.8k, grey 0.6k, light 0.5k, bronze 16. The glass, the louvres and horns, and the white
trim and lattice don't cast shadows, so only the stone and bronze are in the shadow cascades.
